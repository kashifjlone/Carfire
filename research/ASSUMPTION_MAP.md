## 3. Recommended Test Sequence (4-Week Pre-Build De-Risking Plan)

Do **not** write 5–6 months of multi-tenant production code until Steps 1–3 pass:

1. **Week 1 — Legal, Entity & Partner Desk Checks (Zero Engineering Cost):**
   - **A-02 (Entity/Tax):** Consult an Australian corporate/tax adviser on (a) billing B2B from Pakistan (0% GST with ABN + GST confirmation via Stripe/MoR) vs incorporating an AU Pty Ltd (requires resident director under s 201A), and (b) Australian Trade Mark filing if `.com.au` is desired.
   - **A-08 (NSW EGC vs Drive-Away) & A-04 (APP 8 DPA):** Brief an Australian motor-trade/privacy lawyer on NSW EGC vs Drive-Away headline pricing and draft an APP 8 cross-border support clause.
   - **A-03 & A-15 (Feeds & PPSR Brokers):** Send partner inquiry emails to carsales/Nexgate (`lead-api.carsalesnetwork.com.au`), Autotrader/Gumtree (`support@autotradergroup.com.au`), and InfoAgent/MotorWeb to learn partner gates, approval timelines, and minimums.
2. **Weeks 1–3 — Compliant Outreach & 5–10 Dealer Discovery Interviews (A-05, A-01, A-06, A-07, A-09, A-11, A-13):**
   - Build a clean list of 60–80 independent 15–60 car yards in **VIC, NSW, QLD** (prioritising dealers on legacy Bootstrap EasyCars, slow Indiqator, or generic WordPress sites from public registers + site checks).
   - Run Spam Act / Telemarketing Standard compliant outreach (conspicuous-publication emails + landline calls during **4:00 am – 12:00 pm PKT** = 9:00 am – 5:00 pm AEST/AEDT).
   - **Bonus Smoke Test (DealerAssist Pattern):** For 10 target prospects, spin up a static/staging mockup populated with 5–10 of their *actual public vehicles* (`"Here is how your yard looks loading in 0.3s with WhatsApp & mobile filters"`) and attach it to interview invites to test **A-04 (offshore trust)**, **A-05 (response rate)**, and **A-06 (WTP)** simultaneously.
   - Collect **≥3 real anonymised CSV exports** from EasyCars/Virtual Yard/Nexgate (**A-09**).
3. **Week 4 — Go / Scope-Pivot Gate Decision:**
   - **Go with Lean MVP (Storefront + Inventory + CSV + CRM/WhatsApp):** If ≥4/8 dealers accept A$149–$299/mo without P0 Xero/carsales feeds, trust Sydney-hosted offshore support, and confirm CSV exports work.
   - **Pivot MVP Scope:** If dealers universally say *"No switch without Xero Margin Scheme + carsales feed + state police book"*, then either (a) build an **Add-on Storefront + AI WhatsApp/CRM Layer** (Dealer Studio / Dealerloop model, syncing from their existing DMS/Nexgate CSV/feed) rather than a DMS replacement, OR (b) prioritise VIC/QLD/NSW forms + Xero before launch and raise price/budget accordingly.
4. **Weeks 5+ — Concierge Pilot (3–5 Paid/Trial Dealers):**
   - Measure **A-10** (onboarding <3 hrs, monthly support <1.5 hrs/dealer) and **A-12** (mobile browser photo upload adoption) before scaling marketing.
