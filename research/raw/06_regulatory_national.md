## 1. ACCC / Australian Consumer Law (ACL)

### 1.1 Single (Component) Pricing & Drive-Away vs. Plus-On-Roads
- **Summary:** Under **Section 48 of Schedule 2** of the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010* (Cth) (the Australian Consumer Law, ACL), if a dealer advertises or displays a partial or component price for goods ordinarily acquired for personal, domestic, or household use, they must also prominently specify a **single total price** — at least as prominently (size, clarity, visual placement) as any component figure ([ACCC](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEiWHpQeHdi3OYxdRwYL_YR5EUazbMQf_1dn39pm-LlFasIsFuCaopAy-QEQQkRZ2eBcJubONeIE1Za5-9g3fAeM-HrlaH7i8c-xpQ7Pkh-H7NALj0g2Z7XtHgBLoWDh5ka-1p1uaFsbC_s_BPqiSkfH-8Z4nN_Ey6TzzDweddJce0PAjhaaHRXBtq1074c2slGShWoHJHZWo92Y_5yoMioksESg5HVx0b3Hx2ZWhpQmH8jBTI1qDly4SLRRU-t2a7g2UKiju1R1PXn6mohyxFuzNUxpQ==), [Federal Register of Legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.au)).
  - **What "Single Price" (Drive-Away) must include for vehicles:** All mandatory, quantifiable charges at the time of advertisement: base vehicle price (including GST and any applicable Luxury Car Tax), mandatory dealer delivery / pre-delivery fees, and state/territory statutory on-road charges (registration, Compulsory Third Party [CTP] insurance, and motor vehicle stamp duty) ([ACCC](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGx5uoyNiVQzz0OQiQ9PX8dRTl0c31R_IWHDCy10IZXZ2qV0pbbf394oSR6n461XnxJE4Qn50ahA6STbwt_2GXHdV9-5Uv1f7qqWB9U5uSFga4ZCJfssDa-fjdHy7GlhJcBtrWR4z_WxHGJqpopVGzdzCH1II0Rd3pGpfAw_eIYZhFVrtrFzS90n0ldUcAUUnLVbguY5sbqFw==), [QBE](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQFahqLue2Q2KFjjlC0UVaLUPJaRoB-IdpE6DoKeTFbDVB2urDNA-WDg2z6bPrUgmbThkahsr2S8ISZjMvhJmB2cBLwK23gwthTtQPc-6UaUvGBFpJG2tPl1HY77MLTx781WMZWbvW5-3Ij_71Ms), [Consumer Affairs Victoria](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQGQFw9XidaT7eh9jIAQu3F5sxXgrzLh2zjGlY-UasklCN_EPl0rfeotR71YJo5XrvF4_fnFcl-2FGywRJRXnAiEHDSWn60Uc21XwIEVx6Yaa0AxMljlOoqi2ZToUJyQZSFnQizRu1XkkO2aNV11e1S16pFkW7ePR9aY19512A3xWfybAZJ4nmr1p_IVdfLENjEyg2peDaj9z3jYXwmy4XOXcYkk_w==)).
  - **Drive-away vs. Plus On-Roads (variable statutory fees):** Because stamp duty and CTP vary by buyer state, postcode, or concessions, if a single national drive-away price cannot be fixed, the dealer must clearly qualify the jurisdiction (e.g., *"Drive-away in NSW"*) rather than burying mandatory dealer delivery or state fees in fine print. Advertising a headline price that excludes unavoidable dealer delivery or known statutory fees is **drip pricing** and breaches ACL ss 18, 29, and 48 ([ACCC](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQHinSQMqUPtH8mJo02BSaPz4U86l2sopcm1Ao9l8L8gH3fdv_Vyk4-343efJLpCvz5ZcDa3BRrFaWIRS-z1mUp0KA1j4dPZNIN4E5eDBmsc9DfwU_wT1GNv64Eco9PcPYEoGMGUMKpm8SI9P1eVos18k8QgfbQ4xJOXfsxjyLVWRJHWWTSXL30ykgUMuknUqCo221cA-zNc)).
- **Official URLs:**
  - `https://www.accc.gov.au` — *Pricing manual for the motor vehicle industry* ([ACCC redirect](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEIzEG-6YKWkNBJXBmoqhWIPpDpQ19gAfHavSuc2cVCjemrVtzJuGzkSyOKJHN5IR5BzOjTgnoaUYc8PLSPeDs64yqNiipfxR_RRpJrCgGlKew26v7Iz_qwkITeyb2-7hQjTWyl9ZCzXalS-_22ub-VHgL3d5uKSyddJ9ngLM-TtULe_V255qJpaS4No5_1kdGXix2Yaq5szA==)) and component pricing guidance ([ACCC redirect](https://vertexaisearch.cloud.google.com/grounding-api-redirect/AUZIYQEiWHpQeHdi3OYxdRwYL_YR5EUazbMQf_1dn39pm-LlFasIsFuCaopAy-QEQQkRZ2eBcJubONeIE1Za5-9g3fAeM-HrlaH7i8c-xpQ7Pkh-H7NALj0g2Z7XtHgBLoWDh5ka-1p1uaFsbC_s_BPqiSkfH-8Z4nN_Ey6TzzDweddJce0PAjhaaHRXBtq1074c2slGShWoHJHZWo92Y_5yoMioksESg5HVx0b3Hx2ZWhpQmH8jBTI1qDly4SLRRU-t2a7g2UKiju1R1PXn6mohyxFuzNUxpQ==)).

- **Implication for the product:**
  1. **Pricing data model must separate price basis:** Storefront templates and DMS inventory records must explicitly distinguish `price_type` (`drive_away` vs `excl_govt_charges`), store `dealer_delivery_fee` and `state_jurisdiction` (e.g., `"Drive Away in VIC"`), and **never** allow mandatory dealer admin/delivery fees to be hidden behind a lower headline price or added only at an online checkout/enquiry step.
  2. **Prominence enforcement in UI:** When a dealer enters both a vehicle price and a drive-away price, the storefront template must display the single drive-away figure at equal or greater font size/prominence than any component price.
  3. **CSV import mapping:** Imported inventory feeds from legacy DMSs often mix "Excl. Govt Charges" and "Drive Away" prices; the CSV importer must require mapping or flagging `price_type` so imported listings don't mislabel an excl.-on-roads figure as drive-away (or omit required state qualifiers).
- **Confidence:** **High**.
- **Lawyer-needed flag:** **No** for basic storefront pricing fields and prominence rules; **Yes** if the product later calculates automated state-by-state stamp duty/CTP quotes (errors could trigger misleading-price liability).

## 2. Privacy Act 1988, APPs & Notifiable Data Breaches (2024–2026 Reforms)

- **Summary:**

## 5. GST Registration for an Australian-Registered SaaS Business Selling B2B

*(Updated 24 Nov 2026: Founder is operating and developing in Australia through a registered Australian business, so **domestic ATO GST rules** apply rather than non-resident digital supply rules.)*

- **Summary:**
