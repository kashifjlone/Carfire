## 1. Executive Summary: Cross-State Software Architecture Implications

Building a multi-tenant B2B SaaS (inventory/DMS-lite, branded storefront, CRM, later PPSR/Xero/feeds) for independent Australian used-car dealers (~10–80 vehicles, 1–few branches) cannot hard-code a single national compliance workflow:

1. **Jurisdiction-scoped, effective-dated rule profiles are mandatory.** Branch-level state (`NSW | VIC | QLD | ...`)—not just tenant-level—determines which statutory warranty tiers, form templates, cooling-off periods, consignment permissions, and licence-display rules apply.
2. **Storefront "lead-gen website" vs. "online motor dealer" (NSW) is a sharp legal boundary.** Under the *Motor Dealers and Repairers Regulation 2025* (NSW, commenced 1 Sep 2025), simply hosting an enquiry/lead-generation website does **not** trigger the NSW "online motor dealer" regime—whereas completing online vehicle sales on the website **does** require a `.au` domain, 20 business days' advance notice to NSW Fair Trading, publicly accessible Form 5 notices per listing, a 10% pre-delivery deposit cap, and dual inspection opportunities. Software must keep online deposits/checkout **disabled by default** and gate them on verified NSW premises notification (Lawyer Review Required for online reservation deposits).
3. **Price display is governed nationally by the ACL, with state form breakdowns.** Across NSW, VIC, and QLD, ACCC component-pricing rules require any advertised price to show a single total ("drive-away") price at least as prominently as any component (EGC) price, and forbid vague "+ on-road costs". Yet VIC Form 4 and state fee structures still require itemised cash price, stamp duty, transfer, and registration fields internally.
4. **Statutory warranties and cooling-off rules diverge cleanly on deterministic fields, plus edge cases.**
   - **NSW & VIC:** `< 10 years` and `< 160,000 km` → 3 months / 5,000 km (cars).
   - **QLD:** Two-tier: Class A (`<= 10 years` and `< 160,000 km` → 3 months / 5,000 km) AND Class B (`> 10 years` or `>= 160,000 km` → 1 month / 1,000 km), plus restorable/consignment/WOVR exclusions.
   - **Cooling-off:** NSW = 5 pm next business day *only for dealer-linked finance* (Form 12); VIC = 3 clear business days (lost on delivery; greater of \$100 or 1% fee); QLD = 1 business day (ends 5 pm next business day; max \$100 fee; extends to 7 days if Form 12 omitted; excluded for consignment).
5. **Consignment & trust accounts split the three states.** Consignment is **illegal** for VIC LMCTs; **legal and heavily trust-account regulated** in NSW and QLD (next-business-day banking, 14-day disbursement in NSW, annual trust audits, 5–6 year record retention). Do not build generic "consignment" or "deposit collection" UI without state gating.
6. **Electronic stock registers need an immutable audit trail and 5–6+ year retention.** NSW Form 1 and VIC Form 2 allow electronic registers, *provided* amendments/deletions preserve original entries and timestamps (audit log), records can be printed/exported per branch on demand, and retention meets statutory minimums (NSW: 6 years; VIC: 6 years; QLD: 24-hour entry SLA and 5 years for trust/deposit registers).
7. **Written-off vehicles (WOVR) differ sharply.** NSW generally prohibits re-registering repairable write-offs (narrow exemptions only), whereas VIC (VIV) and QLD (WOVI) allow repairable write-offs to be re-registered after strict inspection—requiring clear WOVR disclosure and (in QLD) statutory warranty exclusion.

## 3. National Baselines & Brief Note on Other Jurisdictions

### 3.1 National Rules (All States & Territories)
- **Australian Consumer Law (ACL) Single / Component Pricing (s 48 ACL):** Enforced by ACCC and state fair trading bodies. Any advertised component price (e.g., EGC) requires the single total ("drive-away") price—including GST, dealer delivery, stamp duty, CTP, registration, and LCT if applicable—to be displayed **at least as prominently** (equal or larger font/weight). Prohibits burying mandatory charges in fine print or "+ on-road costs". ([ACCC Cars & Vehicles](https://www.accc.gov.au/by-industry/cars-and-vehicles); [ACCC Motor Vehicle Pricing Manual](https://www.accc.gov.au/system/files/Pricing%20Manual%20for%20the%20motor%20vehicle%20industry.pdf)).
- **ACL Consumer Guarantees vs. State Statutory Warranties:** Automatic consumer guarantees (acceptable quality, fit for purpose, matches description, clear title) apply to all dealer retail sales (excluding public auctions) **in addition to** and **independent of** state statutory warranties—even on > 10-year-old / > 160,000 km cars. Phrases like *"Sold As Is"*, *"No Warranty Express or Implied"*, or extended-warranty terms implying they replace statutory rights are unlawful ([ACCC Cars & Vehicles](https://www.accc.gov.au/by-industry/cars-and-vehicles)).
- **PPSR / NEVDIS:** National Register under *Personal Property Securities Act 2009* (Cth) querying encumbrances, NEVDIS stolen status, WOVR write-off status, and VIN/engine identity ([PPSR Disclaimers](https://www.ppsr.gov.au/about-us/technical-information/conditions-and-terms-use/our-disclaimers)). **Software rule:** Never claim "Guaranteed Clear Title" from a stale or unverified check; store `ppsr_search_number`, `ppsr_checked_at`, and `ppsr_outcome`, and let dealers attach/redact certificates on request.

### 3.2 Other States & Territories (Brief Context for Multi-State Schema)
If the SaaS later expands beyond NSW/VIC/QLD, note that thresholds and cooling-off differ significantly across the remaining 5 jurisdictions (secondary comparison sources — **UNVERIFIED against primary state Acts; do not use for production rules without primary verification**):
- **WA:** Tiered warranty (`< 10 yrs & < 150k km` = 3 mo/5,000 km; `10–12 yrs & 150k–180k km` = 1 mo/1,500 km; excludes `< \$4,000`); **no cooling-off** (up to 5% pre-estimated damages on default). ([Consumer Protection WA](https://www.consumerprotection.wa.gov.au) via search summary — verify directly before WA launch.)
- **SA:** Price-tiered warranty (`>\$6k` = 3 mo/5,000 km up to 15 yrs/200k km; `\$3,001–\$6k` = 2 mo/3,000 km); **2 clear business days** cooling-off (fee: lesser of 2% or \$100; void on delivery).
- **TAS:** Stricter warranty (`< 7 yrs & < 120k km` = 3 mo/3,000 km); **no cooling-off**.
- **ACT:** `< 10 yrs & < 160k km` = 3 mo/5,000 km; **3 clear business days** cooling-off (fee if delivered: lesser of \$250 or 2%).
- **NT:** `< 10 yrs & < 160k km` = 3 mo/5,000 km (waivable via exemption form e.g. Form 12); **no cooling-off**.

## 4. Consolidated Software Requirements Matrix ("Must Support / Must Not Get in the Way Of")

### 4.1 Things Dealer Software MUST Support
1. **Branch-level Licensing & Jurisdictional Profile:** `state` (`NSW | VIC | QLD`), `licence_number`, `licensed_entity_name`, `yard_manager_name` (QLD), `online_sales_model` (NSW), `notified_website_url` (NSW).
2. **State-Compliant Storefront Header/Footer & Listing Disclosures:**
   - NSW: MDLN + licensed name prominently on website and all listings; `.au` domain check + Form 5 link/PDF/QR slot *if* operating as an online motor dealer.
   - VIC: `LMCT {number}` on all ads/pages; `Registration No.` (or `VIN` + `Unregistered`) on every listing.
   - QLD: Auto-display `"No statutory warranty"` and `"No cooling-off period applies"` on listings/feeds whenever applicable.
3. **ACL-Compliant Pricing Model:** Mandatory `total_price_cents` (drive-away) as the primary headline price; `egc_price_cents` optional and strictly subordinate (never larger/bolder); itemised fee breakdown fields (`cash_price`, `stamp_duty`, `transfer_fee`, `rego_fee`, `dealer_delivery`) stored internally to populate VIC Form 4 and future calculators; zero auto-generated `"+ on-road costs"` or `"Sold as is"` copy.
4. **Vehicle Compliance Data Fields:** `vin`, `engine_number`, `chassis_number`, `build_date`, `compliance_date`, `first_registered_date`, `odometer_km` (+ words representation for QLD), `odometer_altered_flag`, `gvm_kg`, `vehicle_category`, `registration_status`, `rego_number`, `acquisition_type` (`owned \| trade_in \| consignment` — block `consignment` in VIC), `wovr_status` (`none \| statutory \| repairable`), `viv_or_wovi_ref`, `rwc_or_safety_cert_no`, `rwc_issued_at`, `ppsr_search_number`, `ppsr_checked_at`, `ppsr_outcome`.
5. **Immutable Audit Trail & Long Retention:** Append-only history on stockbook/register edits (mandatory for NSW Form 1 electronic registers), per-branch register views/exports (NSW, VIC, QLD), 24-hour entry timestamping/reminders (QLD), and 6-year+ retention/export workflows.

### 4.2 Things Dealer Software MUST NOT Get in the Way Of (Anti-Patterns to Avoid)
1. **Do NOT accidentally classify an enquiry-only NSW dealer as an "online motor dealer."** Adding a "Pay \$500 Deposit Online" or "Buy Online Now" checkout widget by default in NSW would push a traditional yard onto a `.au` domain requirement, 20-day Fair Trading notification, 10% cap, dual inspections, and mandatory public Form 5 on every listing. Keep deposits/online checkout **OFF by default**.
2. **Do NOT block valid QLD older/high-km sales or VIC repairable write-offs with NSW-only rules.** QLD dealers legitimately sell Class B warranted cars (> 10 yrs / >= 160,000 km) and WOVI-cleared repairable write-offs; VIC dealers legitimately sell VIV-cleared repairable write-offs; NSW dealers generally cannot retail repairable write-offs. Rules must be per-state, not global blocks.
3. **Do NOT force rigid paper-only assumptions or auto-generate unreviewed legal contracts.** Dealers often already use a DMS, VACC/MTA templates, or state PDFs for actual contracts of sale. Support **PDF/document upload & attachment** per vehicle/deal first (Forms 4/5/6/9/12, RWC, PPSR certificate) before attempting automated form generation.
4. **Do NOT route consignment sale money through standard SaaS/Stripe merchant accounts.** NSW and QLD require licensed dealers to bank consignment proceeds into a regulated statutory trust account by the next business day and undergo annual audits.
